Financial Solutions for Every Need

From women entrepreneurs to farmers and SMEs, MMIC offers tailored Sharia-compliant financing products that unlock potential and drive growth.

Marwo Kaabe

Dedicated financing for women entrepreneurs. Build your business, expand your trade, and achieve financial independence.

FursadMaal

Youth work asset financing — motorbikes, tools, equipment — helping young Somalis build income-generating livelihoods.

SME Finance
