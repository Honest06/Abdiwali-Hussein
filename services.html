Women Financing

Marwo Kaabe

Empowering Women Entrepreneurs

Marwo Kaabe ("Supporting Women" in Somali) is MMIC's flagship product dedicated to women entrepreneurs. It offers accessible, interest-free financing to help women start, grow, and expand their businesses — from small market stalls to established retail shops.

Eligible: Women 18+ with a business idea or existing business
Finance Amount: $200 – $5,000
Repayment Period: 3 – 18 months
Islamic Mode: Murabaha / Qard Hasan
Bonus: Free business coaching & financial literacy

Youth Livelihood

FursadMaal

Youth Work Asset Financing

FursadMaal ("Opportunity Wealth") targets unemployed youth by financing income-generating assets — motorcycles for transport businesses, tools for construction trades, sewing machines for fashion, and equipment for other vocations. Every loan is structured on Islamic finance principles.

Eligible: Youth ages 18–35 seeking work assets
Finance Amount: $300 – $3,000
Repayment Period: 6 – 24 months
Islamic Mode: Murabaha / Ijarah
Assets: Motorcycles, tools, machines, equipment

Technology & Home

E-Fursad

Technology & Home Asset Finance

E-Fursad bridges the digital divide by financing smartphones, laptops, solar panels, televisions, refrigerators, and other essential home and technology assets. Designed for families and individuals seeking to improve their quality of life through ethical installment plans.

Eligible: Individuals & families with stable income
Finance Amount: $100 – $2,000
Repayment Period: 3 – 12 months
Islamic Mode: Murabaha

Business Growth

SME Finance

Small & Medium Enterprise Finance

MMIC's SME Finance product provides growth capital to registered small and medium businesses looking to scale operations, hire staff, expand inventory, or invest in new infrastructure. Designed for entrepreneurs who are ready to take their business to the next level.

Eligible: Registered SMEs with 6+ months operation
Finance Amount: $1,000 – $25,000
Repayment Period: 6 – 36 months
Islamic Mode: Murabaha / Musharakah

Farming

Agriculture Finance

Financing for Smallholder Farmers

MMIC provides seasonal and medium-term financing to smallholder farmers for seeds, fertilizers, irrigation equipment, farm tools, and post-harvest storage. Structured around crop cycles using Islamic Salam contracts to align with farmer cash flows.

Eligible: Smallholder farmers with land access
Finance Amount: $200 – $10,000
Repayment: Aligned with harvest seasons
Islamic Mode: Salam / Murabaha

Pastoralism

Livestock Finance

For Pastoralists & Herders

Somalia's pastoral communities are among the most economically active yet financially excluded. MMIC's Livestock Finance product funds the purchase of goats, cattle, and camels as income-generating assets, using Islamic Murabaha structures.

Eligible: Pastoralists & livestock traders
Finance Amount: $300 – $8,000
Repayment Period: 6 – 18 months
Islamic Mode: Murabaha

Coastal Communities

Fisheries Finance

For Fishing Communities

Somalia's vast coastline supports thousands of fishing families. MMIC provides financing for fishing boats, nets, cold storage equipment, and processing facilities — helping fishers earn more and reduce post-catch losses.

Eligible: Artisanal fishers and fishing cooperatives
Finance Amount: $500 – $15,000
Repayment Period: 6 – 24 months
Islamic Mode: Murabaha / Musharakah

Group Finance

VSLA / SHG Linkage

Village Savings & Loan Association Finance

MMIC partners with Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs) and Self-Help Groups (SHGs) to provide top-up financing to their members. This group-guarantee model reduces risk and extends financial services to the most remote communities.

Eligible: Registered VSLA/SHG groups of 10–25 members
Finance Amount: $100 – $500 per member
Repayment Period: 3 – 12 months
Islamic Mode: Murabaha / Qard Hasan
