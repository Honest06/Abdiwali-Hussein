Client Stories

Stories of Change

Behind every loan is a human story. Here are three lives transformed by MMIC's ethical finance.

Women Empowerment · Mogadishu

Fadumo: From Market Stall to Boutique Owner

Fadumo had been selling fabrics from a small street stall for years, unable to grow due to lack of capital. In 2022, she accessed a $1,200 Marwo Kaabe loan from MMIC. She stocked a larger inventory, moved to a permanent shop, and hired two employees. Today her monthly revenue has tripled.

"MMIC did not just give me money. They believed in me, trained me, and walked with me. I am a businesswoman now."
— Fadumo A., Hodan District, Mogadishu
Youth Livelihood · Baidoa

Ahmed: A Motorcycle, A Career, A Future

Ahmed was 22, educated but unemployed in Baidoa. Through FursadMaal, MMIC financed a motorcycle for him under a Murabaha agreement — no interest, no collateral, just a group guarantee. Within six months, Ahmed was earning $250/month from his transport business and had repaid half his loan.

"I used to feel hopeless. Now I wake up every day with purpose. My family eats better. I am building something."
— Ahmed M., Baidoa
Agriculture · Hudur, Bakool

Hassan: From Subsistence to Market Farmer

Hassan farmed a small plot near Hudur, producing just enough for his family. A $700 Salam agriculture financing from MMIC enabled him to purchase drip irrigation equipment and quality seeds. His yield doubled, and he now sells surplus to Hudur market weekly — doubling his family's income.

"Before MMIC, I farmed to survive. Now I farm to grow. The Salam system was perfect — I received cash before planting and repaid with crops."
— Hassan D., Hudur, Bakool Region
