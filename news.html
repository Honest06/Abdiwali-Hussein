MMIC Opens New Branch in Baidoa to Serve Bay Region Communities

Midnimo Microfinance Institution Company has inaugurated its second regional branch in Baidoa, Bay Region. The new branch will bring Sharia-compliant financial services to over 500,000 residents across Bay Region, with a particular focus on women entrepreneurs, smallholder farmers, and youth seeking work asset financing.

ProgramsNovember 28, 2024

Marwo Kaabe Program Reaches 5,000 Women Entrepreneurs Milestone

MMIC's flagship women financing program, Marwo Kaabe, has now served over 5,000 women entrepreneurs across Somalia, providing over $1.8M in Sharia-compliant financing. A celebration event was held in Mogadishu honoring the program's most successful clients and recognizing their contribution to Somalia's economy.

PartnershipOctober 10, 2024

MMIC Signs $500K Revolving Loan Fund Partnership for Bakool and Bay Regions

A new partnership agreement has been signed to implement a donor-funded revolving loan fund targeting smallholder farmers and livestock keepers across Bakool and Bay regions. The fund will be managed by MMIC with full Sharia compliance and quarterly impact reporting to the donor partner.

ImpactSeptember 5, 2024

FursadMaal Youth Program Creates Over 2,000 Transport Jobs in Mogadishu and Baidoa

MMIC's FursadMaal motorcycle financing product has directly enabled over 2,000 young men and women to establish transport businesses across Mogadishu and Baidoa. An independent impact assessment confirmed that 87% of FursadMaal clients reported increased household income within six months of receiving financing.

EventsAugust 20, 2024

MMIC Launches Free Financial Literacy Training Program Across 3 Regions

MMIC has launched a comprehensive free financial literacy and business coaching program targeting 3,000 clients and community members across Mogadishu, Baidoa, and Hudur. Sessions cover budgeting, savings, Islamic finance principles, and business record-keeping.

MMIC Expands to Hudur, Bringing Finance to Bakool Region's Rural Communities

The inauguration of MMIC's Hudur branch marks a significant step in the institution's mission to reach Somalia's most remote communities. The branch will focus on agriculture finance, livestock finance, and VSLA linkage programs for Bakool region's largely rural population.
