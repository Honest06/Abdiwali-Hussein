About MMIC

Our story, mission, and commitment to ethical financial inclusion in Somalia.

Who We Are

A Mission of Financial Dignity

Midnimo Microfinance Institution Company (MMIC) was established in 2020 with a clear purpose: to deliver ethical, Sharia-compliant financial services to the underserved communities of Somalia.

Operating with transparency, compassion, and Islamic finance principles, we provide micro and small business loans, asset financing, and financial literacy services to women, youth, farmers, SMEs, and rural entrepreneurs who are excluded from conventional banking.

Sharia ComplianceEvery product approved by our Sharia Supervisory Board
Community FirstDecisions rooted in community impact, not profit alone
TransparencyOpen reporting to clients, donors, and regulators

Our Mission

To provide accessible, Sharia-compliant financial services that empower underserved individuals and enterprises in Somalia to build resilient and sustainable livelihoods.

Our Vision

A Somalia where every individual — regardless of gender, geography, or income level — has access to dignified, ethical financial services that unlock their full potential.

Our Values

Integrity. Justice. Inclusion. Innovation. Partnership. These five values guide every decision we make, every product we design, and every client relationship we build.

Our Journey

Milestones & Growth

2020

Founded

MMIC is officially established in Mogadishu with a founding team committed to Sharia-compliant microfinance. First branch opened in Hodan District.

2021

First 500 Clients

MMIC crosses the 500-client milestone, primarily serving women entrepreneurs through the Marwo Kaabe product. First Sharia audit completed successfully.

2022

FursadMaal Launch & Expansion

Launch of FursadMaal youth asset finance program. Expansion into agriculture and livestock finance. Portfolio grows to $500K.

2023

Baidoa Branch & Donor Partnerships

Opening of Baidoa branch in Bay Region. First international donor partnership signed. Total clients reach 8,000. E-Fursad technology finance product launched.

2024

Hudur Branch & Scale-Up

Third branch opened in Hudur. Portfolio surpasses $4.2M. Over 12,500 clients served. VSLA/SHG linkage finance introduced. Financial literacy program scaled to 3 regions.

Sharia Supervisory Board

MMIC's Sharia Supervisory Board comprises qualified Islamic scholars who review, certify, and audit all products and operations to ensure full compliance with Islamic finance principles. Annual Sharia audits are conducted and reports are available to partners upon request.
