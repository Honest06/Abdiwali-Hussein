Partners & Donors

Building Somalia's financial future together — through strategic partnerships and responsible development finance.

Why Partner with MMIC

Your Trusted Implementation Partner in Somalia

MMIC offers international partners a credible, compliant, and community-trusted channel to deliver development finance at scale across Somalia.

Sharia Compliance

All programs are structured within Islamic finance principles and independently certified by our Sharia Supervisory Board — essential for donor alignment with Muslim-majority communities.

Proven Track Record

Since 2020, MMIC has disbursed $4.2M+ to 12,500+ clients across 3 regions with a strong repayment rate. Our portfolio performance data is available upon request.

Robust Reporting

Quarterly and annual impact reports covering portfolio quality, client demographics, SDG indicators, and financial summaries — meeting international donor standards.

Community Trust

MMIC is embedded in local communities. Our loan officers speak the language, understand the culture, and are trusted by clients in ways that external organizations cannot replicate.

Geographic Reach

Branches in Mogadishu, Baidoa, and Hudur — with the capacity to expand to additional regions through new partnerships. We operate where others cannot.

Partnership Models

How We Work with Partners

01

Revolving Loan Fund Management

Donor provides capital; MMIC disburses, manages, monitors, and reports on the fund. Repayments revolve to reach more clients. Full compliance with donor requirements.

02

Grant-Based Programs

MMIC implements grant-funded financial inclusion programs — financial literacy, VSLA support, business coaching — with rigorous M&E and donor reporting.

03

Technical Assistance

MMIC provides field expertise, community engagement, and operational delivery for technical assistance programs targeting financial sector development in Somalia.

04

Co-Investment

DFIs and impact investors can co-invest with MMIC in our loan portfolio. We offer transparent financial reporting and social impact metrics aligned with international standards.
